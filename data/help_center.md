# Shipping times and costs
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US and costs $5.99. It is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.

Orders placed before 1 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

# Tracking your order
When your order ships, we email a tracking link from ShipStation. You can also find tracking under Orders in your ShopLane account.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after the label is created. This is normal and does not mean the package is lost.

# Delayed or lost packages
If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or the package is past its estimated delivery date by more than 3 business days, contact us and we will open a carrier investigation.

If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. Investigations usually take 3-7 business days.

# Changing your shipping address
You can change the shipping address within 1 hour of placing the order from the Orders page. After that, the order is already being packed and the address cannot be changed by our team.

If the order has shipped, you can request a delivery change directly with the carrier using your tracking number.

# Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of purchase from the Orders page, with a full refund. After 1 hour, the order cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery under our return policy.

# Return policy
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all tags.

Final sale items, gift cards, and personalized items cannot be returned. Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise a $6 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

# Refund timelines
Once your return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method.

Card refunds take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank. Store credit refunds are available immediately.

# Exchanges
We offer free exchanges for a different size or color of the same item within 30 days of delivery, if the new item is in stock. Start an exchange from the Orders page. We ship the new item as soon as the original is scanned by the carrier.

# Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within 30 days, contact us within 7 days of noticing the problem with a photo of the damage. We send a free replacement or a full refund, and you do not need to pay for return shipping.

# Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and ShopLane gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery or bank transfers.

# Failed payments and double charges
If a payment fails, your bank may show a temporary authorization hold. Holds are released automatically by your bank within 3-5 business days.

If you see two completed charges for one order, contact us with your order number. Our billing team confirms and refunds duplicate charges within 2 business days.

# Product warranty
Electronics and appliances come with a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear. To make a claim, contact us with your order number and a description or video of the issue.

# Size guide and fit
Every clothing product page has a size chart in inches and centimeters. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up. Fit questions can be answered by our team before you order.

# Escalations and disputes
Our support team cannot respond to legal threats, chargeback disputes, or press inquiries. These are forwarded to our specialist team, which replies within 2 business days.

If you have filed a chargeback with your bank, we cannot also process a refund for the same order until the dispute is closed.
